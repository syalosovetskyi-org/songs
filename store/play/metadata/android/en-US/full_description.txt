Spivanik is a comprehensive offline songbook designed for lovers of Ukrainian music, guitar players, and singers.

Browse a rich catalog of songs spanning traditional folk melodies, classic hits, and modern rock and pop. The app works entirely offline: access lyrics and guitar chords anytime and anywhere without needing an internet connection.

Key Features:
• Rich Catalog: More than 3,000 artists and over 29,000 songs.
• 100% Offline: The entire database is stored on your device, making it perfect for road trips, camping, and jam sessions.
• Guitar Chords: Clean monospace chord alignment above lyrics, with smooth horizontal scrolling for wide chord progressions.
• Instant Smart Search: Fast search across artist names, song titles, and lyric lines simultaneously.
• Ukrainian Language Support: Intelligent search normalization seamlessly handles various apostrophe styles (’ ʼ ' `) and character variations.
• Comfortable Reading: Modern Material 3 interface with dark theme support to reduce eye strain.
• Selectable Text: Easily select and copy lyrics whenever needed.
• Lightweight & Fast: High-performance list rendering and instant navigation.

Enjoy singing your favourite Ukrainian songs with Spivanik!
